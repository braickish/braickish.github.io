Braickish

brack·ish /ˈbrak-ish/

adjective

1.Somewhat salty, especially water that mixes freshwater and seawater; also, unpleasant, distasteful, or unpalatable.

2.(also br·ai·ck·ish) Where AI and human currents mix. Like an estuary, somewhat unpalatable, yet among the most productive places on earth and where the most interesting things tend to grow.
